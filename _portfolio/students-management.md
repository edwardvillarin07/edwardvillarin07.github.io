Most education software is built for administrators. **Students Management** is built for the student who is trying to hold six subjects, three deadlines, and a part-time job in their head at once.

## What it does

- Tracks subjects, assessments, and deadlines in one place
- Surfaces upcoming work and flags what is at risk of slipping
- Encourages a repeatable study routine rather than a one-off cram session
- Keeps the interface simple enough to use on the phone between classes

## Why it matters to us

This is the kind of product we like: unglamorous, high-value, and judged entirely on whether people keep using it after week two. It is also where we practise the discipline of shipping small and shipping often.

## Stack

Application layer in a modern web stack, with a relational data model behind it.
